Motion styles

One short loop per motion style, rendered from deterministic HTML in the default theme with placeholder copy for a fictional app, "Lumen". Every colour, word and image is swappable. Ask your agent for a video and it will plan with these by number, for example "open on 03, explain with 08 and 11, end on 16".

A

The foreground carries the cut. A word or object flies through while the next scene is already waiting.

B

One actor stays. The product, screen or logo keeps its identity from shot to shot.

C

Land, then leave fast. Readable landings, quick exits, no linear moves, no dead holds.

D

Cause, then effect. Every action visibly produces a result, like a click filling a progress bar.
