# Intake: the questions

Goal: in one round trip, learn enough to write a plan the user says yes to. Seven questions, the first six matter most. Ask them together, numbered, each with short examples and the default you will use if they skip it. Never ask more than one follow-up round; after that, decide and say what you decided.

## When to skip

- **Skip the whole intake** when the user already gave: purpose + audience, the message (what it shows or teaches), format/length, and a look (style numbers, a reference, or a clear mood). Go straight to the plan.
- **Ask only the gaps** when some are answered. Restate what you understood in one line so they can correct it.
- **"Just make it" / "you pick"**: use the defaults below, list them in one line each, and go to the plan.
- **Their own product with code or a URL available**: inspect it first (brand colours, fonts, logo, product name, tagline, real screens) and turn questions into confirmations.

## The questions (copy this shape)

> Before I animate anything, a few quick questions so the video actually says what you want. Answer in a word or two each, or say "you pick" for any of them.
>
> 1. **What is it for, and who sees it?** e.g. launch post on X, landing page hero, App Store preview, a 15 s ad, an investor or demo-day intro, onboarding. *Default: a launch/social post for people who have never heard of the product.*
> 2. **What must they get from it?** The one thing they should remember, plus up to 3 things it should show or teach (features, steps, a before/after, a number you can back up). *Default: what it is, the top 3 benefits, how to start.*
> 3. **How should it feel?** Pick one or two: calm and premium · bold and high-energy · clear and trustworthy · playful · futuristic/technical · warm and human · editorial/minimal. And what should they feel at the end (curious, confident, excited, reassured)? *Default: clear and confident, ending curious enough to click.*
> 4. **Where will it play, and how long?** Feed 4:5 · Reels/TikTok/Shorts 9:16 · YouTube/website 16:9 · square 1:1; 6 s, 15 s, 30 s or 60 s; sound on, or captions for sound-off? *Default: 4:5, 20 s, sound-off friendly with a music bed.*
> 5. **What should I use from your brand?** Logo, colours, fonts, screenshots or product shots, a site or repo I can pull them from. Anything to avoid (words, claims, competitor names, legal lines)? *Default: I'll pull from your site/repo if I can, otherwise a neutral theme you can swap later.*
> 6. **What should they do at the end?** The exact call to action and URL or handle (try it free, download, join the waitlist, follow). *Default: product name + URL on the end card.*
> 7. *(optional)* **Anything you love or hate?** A video, brand or style you'd like it to feel like, or styles from the gallery by number. Voiceover? Deadline?

## Reading the answers

| They said | Means for the plan |
|---|---|
| Launch / social post | Hook in the first 1.5 s, one idea per beat, end card with CTA, works muted |
| Landing page hero | Seamless loop, no hard CTA, calmer motion, poster frame matters |
| App Store / product demo | Real screens are the hero; explain styles (08, 09, 11, 12), short captions |
| Ad (6-15 s) | One message, product visible by second 2, CTA on screen for 2 s or more |
| Explainer / onboarding | 3-5 steps, one persistent object, slower reading pace |
| Investor / demo day | Problem, product, proof, ask; editorial and calm styles |
| "Premium", "expensive" | Long eases, slow pushes, few elements: 04, 15, 16, 20, 22 |
| "Energetic", "fun" | Beat-cut type, squash and stretch, snappy exits: 03, 18, 26, 27, 28 |
| "Trustworthy", "clear" | Grids, steps, rails, materializing results: 08, 09, 11, 12, 17 |
| "Futuristic", "technical" | Shaders and fields: 19, 21, 23, 24, 10 |

## Feelings vocabulary (use their words back to them)

Calm · premium · confident · bold · urgent · playful · warm · human · clever · technical · futuristic · editorial · cinematic · trustworthy · celebratory · mysterious.

Each feeling maps to motion: calm = long ease-outs, few moving parts, slow drift; bold = big type, fast cuts on the beat, strong contrast; playful = overshoot, squash and stretch, bright accents; premium = restraint, glare and light, generous space; technical = grids, precise alignment, generative texture.

## Done when

You can fill every row of the plan header (goal, audience, feeling, format, length, CTA) without guessing, or you have said which ones you defaulted.
