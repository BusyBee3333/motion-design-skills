# Planning: story, timing, the plan

## Story shapes (pick one, then fit beats to the length)

| Purpose | Beats |
|---|---|
| **Launch / announce** (15-30 s) | Hook (the claim or the pain, 1.5-3 s) → Reveal the product (2-3 s) → 2-3 benefits, one per beat (2.5-4 s each) → Proof (a real number, a real screen, 2-3 s) → End card + CTA (2.5-3 s) |
| **Explainer / how it works** (20-45 s) | Question the viewer has → Step 1, 2, 3 with one persistent object moving through them → The result → CTA |
| **Feature spotlight** (8-15 s) | The task before (slow, annoying) → the feature doing it (fast, satisfying) → name of the feature + CTA |
| **Ad** (6-15 s) | Product visible by second 2 → one benefit → CTA held at least 2 s |
| **Brand sting / logo** (3-6 s) | Abstract build → logo lockup → hold |
| **Hero loop** (website, 6-10 s) | One continuous seamless motion, no CTA, poster frame is the design |
| **Investor / demo day** (30-60 s) | Problem → why now → product → traction you can back → team/ask |

Rules of thumb: one idea per beat; the hook decides whether anyone sees beat 2; show the real product as early as the story allows; end on the CTA, not a montage.

## Timing math

- **Reading time** for a beat with text: `0.6 s + 0.3 s per word` at minimum, then add the motion in and out. 6 words ≈ 2.4 s readable + ~0.6 s of motion = 3 s beat.
- **Max words on screen**: hook 2-5, benefit 3-8, caption 12, end card name + 1 line + URL.
- **Transitions**: 0.35-0.6 s. Entrances land with ease-out; exits run about 70% of the entrance time.
- **Total length** = sum of beats; for social, under 20 s unless the story needs more. Show the CTA for at least 2 s.
- **Speed**: social hooks cut every 1-2 s for the first 3 s, then settle to 2.5-4 s beats. Premium films hold 3-5 s per beat with a slow push.
- **Sound-off**: every beat must make sense muted (on-screen words or burned-in captions). If there is a voiceover, words on screen are keywords, not the full script.

## Choosing styles

Open `style-catalog.md`. Choose by **job** first (hook, explain, show product, transition, end card), then **feeling**. For each beat write the style number and why in five words. Prefer:

- 3-6 different styles in a 15-30 s video; repeating a style for parallel beats (three benefits) reads as a system, not laziness.
- One **persistent hero** (the product card, a screen, the logo, a token) that survives across beats.
- **Foreground transitions**: the outgoing word or object carries the cut (styles 02, 10, 14, 18, 01) instead of a generic fade.
- An end card from 16, 15 or 25 unless the user's brand calls for something else.

## The plan template (show this to the user)

```
VIDEO PLAN: <working title>
Goal: <one line: what the viewer should think/do after>
Audience: <who, where they see it>
Feeling: <2 words> → ends <feeling>
Format: <ratio>, <length> s, <sound on / sound-off + captions>, <music mood>
Look: theme <name or their colours>, fonts <display / body>, art <their screenshots / logo / neutral>
CTA: "<exact words>" <url/handle>

| # | Time | Viewer sees | On-screen words | Style | Into next |
|---|------|-------------|-----------------|-------|-----------|
| 1 | 0.0-2.5 | ... | "..." | 03 Kinetic Type | word flies through lens |
| 2 | ... |

Defaults I chose: <list, one line each>
Open questions: <only if any>
```

Ask: "Good to build, or change anything?" Build after a yes. Small changes later (words, colours, a beat's length) do not need a new plan.

## Worked example: "Make an animated video for my software"

After the intake: a task app called "Tally", launch post on X and LinkedIn, audience = small-team leads, show that it turns messy notes into assigned tasks, feeling = clear and confident, 4:5, 20 s, sound-off with a light music bed, CTA "Try Tally free · tally.app".

```
| # | Time | Viewer sees | On-screen words | Style | Into next |
| 1 | 0-2.5 | Big words cut on the beat | "Meeting notes. / Nobody acts." | 03 Kinetic Type | last word zooms past lens |
| 2 | 2.5-7 | Their real app screen assembles region by region | "Tally turns notes into tasks" | 08 Materializing Result | screen slides up |
| 3 | 7-12 | Three steps fold one into the next | "Paste / Assign / Done" | 11 Perspective Fold | fold strip becomes rail |
| 4 | 12-16 | Tasks flow along rails into the team's board | "Everyone knows what's next" | 09 Persistent Rails | colour-field wipe |
| 5 | 16-20 | Logo lockup on a colour field | "Try Tally free · tally.app" | 16 Colour-Field Lockup | hold |
```
