# 27 Pixel Resolve
**Feeling:** playful, retro-tech, snappy, game-like
**Good for:** game-y social hooks, "loading → reveal" moments, retro-tech accents, transitions that turn any object into pixels and back
**Avoid when:** the brand is elegant or quiet, or the piece is long-form (the shuffle is busy)
**Mechanism:** a 30 px grid on canvas. About 330 square pixels shuffle in grid hops (20 hops per loop, 90 ms slides) in a theme palette, then snap cell by cell into a pixel-mosaic showcase case. Each target colour is sampled from a function that approximates the kit case. The snap is a Manhattan move (x leg, then y leg, out-expo, 160 ms each) and the mosaic stacks bottom to top like a falling-block game; each pixel flashes white and pops 1.35→1 (back-out). An accent scan line then resolves the crisp DOM case top to bottom with a clip-path. The hold carries a glare sweep and a 3% push. A reverse scan returns the mosaic, and the pixels hop off on arcs back into the shuffle field. An 8-bit foil ∞ blinks above, and the headline types on with a block cursor, then backspaces out. Pixel colours are re-read from the theme whenever it changes.
**Timing:** 6 s loop. Shuffle 0–0.55 s, build 0.55–2.0 s, scan resolve 2.08–2.5 s, type-on 2.35–3.1 s, hold to 3.75 s, de-res 3.85–4.2 s, pixels pop off 4.25–5.6 s. Poster 3.3 s.
**Swap points:** COPY `eyebrow` (≤ 4 words), `lines` (2 typed lines ≤ ~14 chars; last word of each line in foil), `sub` (≤ 9 words), `caseEyebrow`, `caseTitle`, `caseNumber`, `cardName`. Art: `<div class="art" style="--art:url(your-image.png)">` on the crisp case card (the pixel phase stays a generated approximation; edit `slabColor()` to match your art). Roles: --warm/--warm-lo card body, --primary edge stripe, foil stops for art and ∞, --accent scan line.
**Adapting:** to pixelate another object, rewrite `slabColor(u,v)` for its silhouette and change `SX/SY/SW/SH`. For 16:9 raise `GW` and keep `CS` at 30 px. Use the de-res half alone (3.85–5.6 s) as an exit transition.
