# 28 Foil Confetti
**Feeling:** celebratory, joyful, punchy, festive
**Good for:** success or "done" beats, launch end cards, milestone and thank-you moments
**Avoid when:** the message is serious or the screen must stay calm for reading (confetti crosses the text)
**Mechanism:** 190 deterministic pieces on two canvases (behind and in front of the showcase case): foil rects, curled ribbons and 26 four-point sparkle stars. Positions use closed-form linear-drag ballistics (k=3, g=980, terminal ≈330 px/s) plus a growing sinusoidal flutter. Each piece has a 3D flip (scaleY=|cos|) that also drives a foil brightness shimmer, and spin that decays over time. A stamp slams down with back-out (k=3.2) and a wobble while the case dips in anticipation (−3.5%) and then punches with a damped spring. A bloom flash expands behind it. Confetti then gathers back into the case (in-cubic, staggered 0.5 s, small arc) so the loop resets.
**Timing:** 5 s loop. Anticipation 0.38–0.62 s, stamp impact 0.62 s, burst 0.62–3.3 s, headline pops 1.05 s (80 ms stagger), sub 1.4 s, stamp and text out 3.55–3.9 s, gather 3.35–4.85 s. Poster 1.9 s.
**Swap points:** COPY `stamp` (≤ ~8 chars), `stampSmall`, `eyebrow` (≤ 3 words), `words` (2–3 short words on one line, last in foil), `sub` (≤ 2 short sentences), `caseEyebrow`, `caseTitle`, `caseNumber`, `cardName`. Art: `<div class="art" style="--art:url(your-image.png)">` on the case card. Roles: confetti uses --accent, --pop, --warm, --text, --primary-hi, --accent-lo; --primary/--primary-lo the stamp; --accent/--accent-lo the card body.
**Adapting:** remove the stamp for a pure confetti end card. For 16:9 widen the spawn spread (`x0`) and lower `v`. In a longer video, trigger the burst on a hard cut and skip the gather by ending before 3.35 s.
