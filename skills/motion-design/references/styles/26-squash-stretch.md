# 26 Squash & Stretch
**Feeling:** playful, bouncy, friendly, characterful
**Good for:** playful explainer beats ("you're in", "approved"), sticker or badge moments, social cut-downs that need personality
**Avoid when:** the tone is serious or luxury, or the object must not be distorted (product photos, logos with strict usage rules)
**Mechanism:** a custom two-part pass (body + hinged stub, both masked with perforation notches) falls on an arc, stretched by speed. It squashes on impact (scaleY .78, X compensated by sy^-0.75, origin at the bottom), stretches on the rebound and bounces twice (150 px, 44 px) with a damped tilt. The stub carries follow-through: a damped spring rotation about the perforation after every impact. A stamp drops toward camera, slams down (squash .88, then back-out k=2.4) and sends a 10-stroke tick burst and a damped camera nudge. Accent ripple rings and a contact shadow scale with height. To exit, the pass anticipates (squash .8), launches up and out stretched, and re-enters from the top on the loop.
**Timing:** 5 s loop. Fall 0–0.5 s, contacts 0.5/1.0/1.32 s (each 60–120 ms), stamp 1.6–2.15 s, words pop 1.95 s (70 ms stagger), idle breathe 2.2–4.0 s, anticipation 4.30–4.52 s, launch 4.52–4.86 s. Poster 2.9 s.
**Swap points:** COPY `eyebrow` (≤ 3 words), `passEyebrow`, `passTitle` (2 lines ≤ 6 chars, `<br>` between), `stamp` (≤ ~11 chars), `stampSmall`, `lines` (2 lines of 2 words, last word in foil), `sub` (≤ 9 words). Art: the stub mark is the `#infp` SVG path; replace it with your icon. Roles: --primary stub/stamp, --text/--text2 pass body, --accent ripples and burst, --warm burst.
**Adapting:** the bounce works on any DOM object; move the squash origin to its bottom edge. Keep contacts at 60–120 ms or it reads as rubber. In a longer video, cut on the launch frame (4.6 s) into the next scene.
