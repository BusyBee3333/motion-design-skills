# 22 Reeded Glass
**Feeling:** premium, tactile, refined, expensive
**Good for:** product-ad reveals of a hero object, launch title cards, glass wipes between two scenes
**Avoid when:** the copy must be readable from the first frame (it starts behind the glass), or on low-power playback where WebGL is unavailable
**Mechanism:** the full scene (backdrop light blobs, showcase case, type) is drawn once to a 2x 2D canvas and uploaded as a texture; a WebGL2 shader slides a fluted-glass panel across it. Each 34 px flute samples a magnified, bowed sliver around its own centre with per-channel dispersion (R/G/B at different magnifications = foil fringes), a 5-tap frost blur, ridge shading, dark grooves, a thin rim light, a broad sheen and bright bevelled panel edges. When clear, a glare band sweeps the case and the camera pushes 3.5%. The texture and shader colour uniforms are rebuilt whenever fonts load or the theme changes.
**Timing:** 5.6 s loop. Glass slides off right 0.45→1.95 s (premium), sharp hold with glare 1.75→3.45 s, glass re-enters from left 3.75→5.15 s (inOutCubic), short covered hold. Poster 1.0 s = half refracted, half sharp.
**Swap points:** COPY `eyebrow` (≤ 3 words), `headline` (≤ ~16 chars, one line at 132 px), `sub` (≤ 6 words), `caseEyebrow`, `caseTitle` (≤ 10 chars), `caseNumber` (2 chars), `cardName`. Art: set `HERO_IMG` at the top of the script to any image path (drawn object-fit: cover into the card art window). Roles: --primary and --accent drive the backdrop lights and glass sheen, --text is the glass highlight, --warm/--warm-lo the card body, the foil stops (--accent/--primary-hi/--pop/--warm) the headline.
**Adapting:** use the glass pass alone as a transition: draw scene A to the texture, swap to scene B while fully covered. For 16:9, widen the scene canvas and keep the flute width at ~34 px so the ribs read the same. Lengthen the sharp hold rather than slowing the slide; the slide is what sells it.
