# 23 Halftone Glyphs
**Feeling:** technical, printed, crafted, data-rich
**Good for:** "how it works" or data sections, transitions between an object and abstract texture, print-meets-tech brand moments
**Avoid when:** the object has fine detail that must stay legible (a 10 px cell grid eats small type), or the brand is soft and organic
**Mechanism:** WebGL2 shader on a 10 px cell grid. Each cell reads a looping domain-warped fbm field (noise sampled on a closed circle) and the pass artwork (2D canvas texture sampled at the cell centre) and blends between them with a per-cell staggered resolve (distance from centre + hash). Dense noise cells print as 3x5 mono glyphs that re-roll 12x per loop; everything else is a halftone dot sized by luminance (dark text = tiny dots, light surface = full dots, the stub keeps its primary colour). A print-pass scan row brightens the pass during the hold. The pass texture and colour uniforms are rebuilt whenever fonts load or the theme changes.
**Timing:** 5.4 s loop. Resolve 0.7→2.1 s (premium, staggered), headline 1.75→2.4 s (outExpo), scan 2.0→3.5 s, type out 3.5→3.85 s, dissolve 3.65→4.85 s (inOutCubic). Poster 2.75 s.
**Swap points:** COPY `eyebrow` (≤ 3 words), `headline` (≤ ~20 chars, one line), `sub` (≤ 8 words), `tagLeft` / `tagRight` (corner labels), `passLines` (2 words ≤ 6 chars, printed big on the pass). Art: replace `drawPass()` with any 2D drawing (logo, product silhouette, screenshot via drawImage) at the `TX` rect; high-contrast art reads best. Roles: --bg is the paper between dots, --primary/--primary-hi/--accent tint the noise field, --text the light parts of the pass.
**Adapting:** change `CS` (cell size) for coarser or finer print; keep it ≥ 8 px at 1080 wide. For 16:9 widen `TX` and the resolve centre. In a longer video, hold `form` at 1 for extra beats and let the scan repeat.
