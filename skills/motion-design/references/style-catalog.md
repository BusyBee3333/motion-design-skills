## Openers and brand moments

| # | Style | Good for | Feels | Avoid when | How it moves |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | [Liquid Morph](styles/01-liquid-morph.md) | Openers, brand stings, and turning one object into another. | fluid, soft, premium, alive | the piece needs hard, technical or data-driven energy; the goo reads as playful | Liquid blobs merge into the shape of the showcase case, the real case lands, then it melts back. |
| 03 | [Kinetic Type](styles/03-kinetic-type-beats.md) | Hooks in the first 3 seconds and music-cut social edits. | loud, rhythmic, punchy, graphic | the message needs more than ~2 words per beat, or the tone is calm or luxury | Huge words swap in place on the beat with pops, mask reveals and colour slams. |
| 07 | [Artifact Ring](styles/07-artifact-ring.md) | One line of copy surrounded by the things it's about. | orbiting, dimensional, confident, airy | the copy is longer than ~4 short words, or you need the objects themselves to be read | Passes, cases and chips orbit a tilted ring while the sentence builds word by word. |
| 16 | [Colour-Field Lockup](styles/16-colorfield-lockup.md) | End-card lockups with a wordmark and URL. | bold, warm, premium, confident | the wordmark is long (more than ~5 wide letters) or the scene must stay text-dense | A wordmark assembles from split halves over an orbiting colour field, then the field swallows it. |

## Explaining how it works

| # | Style | Good for | Feels | Avoid when | How it moves |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 08 | [Materializing Result](styles/08-materializing-result.md) | Reveal a result or summary screen step by step without a real UI. | precise, satisfying, calm, premium | the message is a single word or number; the dot gather needs a layout worth materializing | Dots gather into a card, then the header and 3 rows wipe in with spring checkmarks; reverses by scattering. |
| 09 | [Persistent Rails](styles/09-persistent-rails.md) | Explain two inputs flowing into one destination. | clear, flowing, steady, confident | you have more than two sources or need numbers on screen; the layout is built for exactly two lanes | Fixed glowing rails carry drops from two source chips into a liquid-filled hub while the headline swaps over 3 beats. |
| 11 | [Perspective Fold](styles/11-perspective-fold.md) | 3-step how-it-works sequences. | tidy, tactile, confident, rhythmic | steps need more than a headline and one short line, or you have more than 3-4 steps in 6 s | Panels hinge back into a glowing floor strip and the next panel rises out of it, with a progress rail below. |
| 12 | [Step Carousel](styles/12-carousel-emphasis.md) | Ordered lists of 3-7 short words (steps, features). | energetic, ordered, bold, rhythmic | labels are long phrases; the drum only reads with single short words | Tilted 3D word drum snaps item by item; the active word glows and the background hue follows it. |
| 13 | [Camera Journey](styles/13-camera-journey.md) | A whole flow in one continuous shot; roadmaps and overviews. | spatial, guided, smooth, cinematic | each step needs a long read; holds are only 0.8 s | One camera glides across 4 stations on a lit rail, with zoom dips between holds. |
| 17 | [Swiss Grid](styles/17-swiss-grid.md) | Hard-cut rhythm, counts and "same every time" proof beats. | precise, rhythmic, editorial, confident | you need soft, emotional motion or real data (the numeral is a beat counter, not a statistic) | A 4x3 Swiss grid lights one cell per beat while a giant numeral counts. |

## Showing the product

| # | Style | Good for | Feels | Avoid when | How it moves |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 04 | [Showcase Hero](styles/04-acrylic-slab-hero.md) | Making one object feel physical and premium. | premium, physical, calm, expensive | you need to show several items or explain steps; it is a single-object hero | A 3D turn on a measured curve, with glare and edge light that show the angle. |
| 05 | [Exploded Layers](styles/05-exploded-layers.md) | Product anatomy: showing what something is made of. | technical, precise, credible, premium | the object has no meaningful layers, or labels would need more than a few words | Case, label, card and inner frame separate with labelled callouts, then close up again. |
| 06 | [Stack and Fan](styles/06-stack-fan.md) | Collection reveals and showing variety. | satisfying, abundant, tactile, polished | you only have one item, or the items need to be read in detail | Cards stack up, fan out as the camera pulls back, then collapse to one. |
| 15 | [Velvet Standard](styles/15-velvet-standard.md) | Calm luxury spotlights and expensive end cards. | calm, luxurious, expensive, unhurried | you need energy, fast information, or more than two short captions | A showcase case sways in a hairline frame while wide-tracked captions cross. |

## Transitions

| # | Style | Good for | Feels | Avoid when | How it moves |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 02 | [Fly-Through](styles/02-foreground-flythrough.md) | Going from a big claim to the proof in one cut. | bold, fast, cinematic, confident | the headline is long or needs to be read slowly, or the scene underneath has nothing worth revealing | The headline speeds through the lens while the destination scene is already waiting underneath. |
| 10 | [Logo Portal](styles/10-logo-portal.md) | Logo open or close; a brand mark that opens into the product world. | hypnotic, playful, premium, endless | the viewer must read body copy; text is only readable during the short holds | Seamless Droste zoom through a pass's punched hole into a hub scene, then into the mini pass inside it. |
| 18 | [Tile Wipe](styles/18-tile-wipe.md) | Chapter breaks in step-by-step explainers. | punchy, graphic, rhythmic, playful | the mood is quiet or the cut needs to be invisible | A diagonal cascade of foil and colour tiles flips one chapter card to the next. |

## Abstract and generative

| # | Style | Good for | Feels | Avoid when | How it moves |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 14 | [Recursive Zoom](styles/14-recursive-zoom.md) | Hypnotic endless sting behind a fixed caption. | hypnotic, endless, calm, premium | the scene needs to change or tell steps; it is one idea on repeat | A case holds a room whose pedestal holds the same case; constant exponential push, seamless. |
| 19 | [Liquid Chrome](styles/19-liquid-chrome.md) | Abstract hero openers behind a headline. | sleek, futuristic, luxurious, hypnotic | you need to show a real product or UI, or render time is tight (WebGL, ~seconds per frame) | A chrome liquid surface where a mark rises as an iridescent tube, then sinks back (WebGL2). |
| 20 | [Silk Ribbons](styles/20-silk-ribbons.md) | Slow, elegant backdrops for a product. | elegant, soft, premium, flowing | the message is urgent or the scene must render fast (two WebGL passes) | Folding silk ribbons of light drift behind a levitating showcase case (WebGL2). |
| 21 | [Aura Bloom](styles/21-aura-bloom.md) | Heartbeat or "it's live" pulse moments. | alive, warm, rhythmic, magnetic | the scene needs stillness or must render quickly (WebGL) | Aura rings bloom off a pass on a lub-dub beat over a mesh gradient (WebGL2). |
| 22 | [Reeded Glass](styles/22-reeded-glass.md) | Premium product reveals and glass-wipe transitions. | premium, tactile, refined, expensive | the copy must be readable from the first frame (it starts behind the glass), or on low-power playback where WebGL is unavailable | Fluted glass slides across a hero shot and reveals it sharp, with RGB dispersion (WebGL2). |
| 23 | [Halftone Glyphs](styles/23-halftone-glyphs.md) | Data and "how it works" beats; print-meets-tech textures. | technical, printed, crafted, data-rich | the object has fine detail that must stay legible (a 10 px cell grid eats small type), or the brand is soft and organic | An object prints itself out of a living halftone and glyph field, then dissolves back (WebGL2). |
| 24 | [Flow Field](styles/24-flow-field.md) | Calm generative openers that resolve into a mark. | calm, organic, hypnotic, generative | you need a crisp product shot or the message needs reading in the first second | Thousands of drifting particle streaks gather into a glowing mark, hold, and release. |

## Playful

| # | Style | Good for | Feels | Avoid when | How it moves |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 | [Type Ring](styles/25-type-ring.md) | Editorial titles and end cards. | editorial, confident, rhythmic, crafted | the ring copy is long (it must fit the circumference) or the format is very wide and short | Rings of display and mono type counter-rotate around a hero object. |
| 26 | [Squash and Stretch](styles/26-squash-stretch.md) | Playful "you're in" or "approved" beats. | playful, bouncy, friendly, characterful | the tone is serious or luxury, or the object must not be distorted (product photos, logos with strict usage rules) | A pass drops, squashes, bounces and gets stamped, then jumps away. |
| 27 | [Pixel Resolve](styles/27-pixel-resolve.md) | Game-y hooks and loading-to-reveal moments. | playful, retro-tech, snappy, game-like | the brand is elegant or quiet, or the piece is long-form (the shuffle is busy) | Shuffling pixels snap into a mosaic of the hero, a scan line resolves it crisp, then it de-rezzes. |
| 28 | [Foil Confetti](styles/28-foil-confetti.md) | Success and launch celebrations. | celebratory, joyful, punchy, festive | the message is serious or the screen must stay calm for reading (confetti crosses the text) | A stamp slams onto a hero object and foil confetti bursts, flutters and gathers back. |
